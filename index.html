BEHIND THE BEAN

The Roasting & Extraction Process

From high-altitude shade farms to your ceramic cup—our 3-step obsession with perfection.

01. Direct Trade
1. Ethical Farm Direct Sourcing

We work directly with smallholder farmers in Huila, Yirgacheffe, and Antigua, paying 40% above fair-trade minimums for top 1% harvest lots.

02. Drum Precision
2. Precision Small-Batch Roasting

Roaster curves monitored live via thermal sensors on our cast-iron San Franciscan drum roaster to highlight natural floral and fruit notes.

03. Surgical Bar
3. Precise Pressure Extraction

Customized Synesso MVP machines engineered to control pre-infusion pressure profiles, extracting ideal balance and crema density.
